Yamal scored nine goals in September. But that is only half the story.

Barcelona says he also added seven assists. Sixteen goal contributions in one month.

Here is the detail that matters: he delivered at least two goal contributions in every Barcelona appearance.

Five games. Six goals, six assists. So the story goes beyond one spectacular performance.

Then he carried it into the international break, scoring against England and twice against Croatia.

According to Barcelona, this was his most productive month yet for goals and assists.

My takeaway? Judge him as a finisher and a creator. The assists change the picture.

What matters more when you judge a winger: scoring goals, or creating them?
