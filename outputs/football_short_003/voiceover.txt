Raphinha scored a hat-trick—but the three goals tell the better story.

Against Sevilla, he scored with his right foot, then his head, then his left foot.

That is what football calls a perfect hat-trick.

And here is the bigger change: Barcelona says Flick has moved him from the wing into the number-nine role.

By September twentieth, he had fourteen goals in eight appearances across all competitions.

That does not prove the position change explains everything. But it is a strong start for a winger playing up front.

Would you keep Raphinha as a striker—or move him back out wide?
