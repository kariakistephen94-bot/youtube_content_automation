## Post 2 — Practical automation

Your first AI automation project doesn't need to run your whole business.

Start with one handoff that keeps getting dropped.

For example:
New inquiry → capture details → draft a response → route to the right person → record the outcome.

Use rules for predictable steps. Use AI where language or messy information needs interpretation. Keep a person involved where judgment matters.

Then measure:
• Time to first response
• Missing information
• Manual corrections
• Time spent per inquiry

If those improve, expand the workflow.

What repetitive handoff slows your team down most?
